Pixel Raconte Publisher

Internal tool of an independent creator · not a public service

Pixel Raconte Publisher is a private command-line tool. It schedules the creator's own short educational videos on their own three YouTube channels:

  • Pixel Raconte (French)
  • Curio Box (English)
  • Orbopolis (satisfying simulations)

What it does

  • Uploads a finished video with its title, description, tags and thumbnail.
  • Sets the scheduled publication date, so YouTube publishes the video at the planned time.
  • Reads the statistics of these same videos (views, likes, retention) to improve the next ones.

It is used only by the channel owner, who signs in with Google (OAuth) once per channel. It has no other users and accesses no data of other YouTube users.
